Bachelor of Computer Applications

A professional undergraduate program designed to build a strong foundation in computer science, programming, software development and IT skills.

About BCA

Course Duration

3 Years (6 Semesters)

Course Focus

Programming, Software Development, Databases, Web Technologies & Computer Networks

Why BCA?

Ideal for students aiming for careers in IT, software engineering, data and technology fields.

Eligibility Criteria

Academic Qualification

  • 10+2 from a recognized board
  • Mathematics/Computer Science preferred

Minimum Marks

Typically 45% – 50% (varies by university)

Admission Process

Merit-based or entrance exams depending on institution
